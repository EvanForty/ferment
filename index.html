    From seed to sale

    Every batch runs through the same five stages. Patience pays: the longer a crock ferments, the better it sells.

    1. 1

      Farm

      Plant napa cabbage, chili peppers, garlic, scallions and more. Each crop has its own grow time and yield.

    2. 2

      Craft

      Combine ingredients into kimchi recipes. Rare combinations produce rare recipes worth more at market.

    3. 3

      Ferment

      Seal the batch and let it work in real time. Longer fermentation unlocks higher-quality kimchi and bigger rewards.

    4. 4

      Earn

      Sell finished kimchi to NPC restaurants and fill daily orders to earn $FERMENT.

    5. 5

      Upgrade

      Spend $FERMENT on bigger farms, better barrels, factories, and automated production lines.

    Automated fermentation factories

    Build a factory and it keeps producing kimchi while you’re away. Factories unlock rare recipes and let you scale far past what hand-sealed crocks can manage.

    Where upgrade spending goes

    Every $FERMENT spent on upgrades is split three ways the moment it’s paid.

    50%

    Burned permanently, removing it from supply.

    30%

    Returned to the player reward pool that pays out for orders and sales.

    20%

    Funds development, seasonal events, and the treasury.

    The reward pool is capped and emissions are paced, so rewards are paid from a fixed budget topped up by player spending rather than minted without limit.

    The flywheel

    More players means more production. More production drives more upgrades. Upgrades burn $FERMENT and refill the reward pool, which gives the token more use and draws in more players.
